## MODE — why each event is classed as it is

- Event 1 is **transacts**: "does hereby grant and release" matches the
  operative-language pattern measured 100% operative on the 23,282-clause
  bench. The world changed.
- Event 2 is **observes**: taking "subject to" recites a pre-existing state.
  The tenancies and the arrears existed before this deed and after it; the
  deed changed neither. Only a `transacts` event may assert that state
  changed, and this one may not.

## INTERNAL vs EXTERNAL — the inference this deed sets up (NOT a fact)

A nominal price between apparently unrelated parties is the classic signature
of an INTERNAL transfer — but internal-ness is an INFERENCE for 05 Derivations,
never an extraction fact. Extraction's duty is to capture the evidence that
lets derivation decide. The tests, and what this document says:

| test | evidence here | reads |
|---|---|---|
| name match | PROPER REALTY CORP vs ELISEO RAMIREZ | no match |
| entity match | corporation -> natural person | no match |
| mailing address match | 1860 Broadway vs 522 Academy Street | no match |
| signature / officer match | LEONARD SOLOMON signs as President for the grantor; the grantee signs nothing | no match |
| corporate-authority recital | unanimous written consent of ALL stockholders (BCL s909 form, used when a corporation parts with substantially all its assets) | **suggests a liquidation or distribution** |

**Verdict: UNRESOLVED, leaning internal-by-structure.** No identity evidence
links the parties, yet the price is nominal and the grantor is winding down its
holdings — the shape of a distribution to a shareholder rather than a sale to a
stranger. What would settle it: whether Eliseo Ramirez was a stockholder or
officer of Proper Realty Corp (not in this document), or a later instrument
naming both. Recorded as a signal with its reasoning; never written as a fact.
