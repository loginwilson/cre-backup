# Documents surveyed for framework v1-B

Every page image in each package below was opened and read during isolated drafting. The document id is the package id; the lesson records the rule pressure it supplied, not an extraction result for production use.

| document id | registration type | what it taught the draft |
|---|---|---|
| 2002122000002001 | SATISFACTION OF MORTGAGE | A lien satisfaction can expressly discharge the mortgage while a quoted $366,000 is only the referenced original principal; the 2002 execution date and 2003 filing date also forced an explicit no-recording-date fallback rule. |
| 2002122700153001 | DEED | The operative deed's nominal $10 and the executed transfer report's $525,500 full sale price and $23,039 assessed value are different quantity kinds and source classes, while an attached smoke-detector affidavit needs its own assertion test. |
| 2002122700120001 | DEED OTHER | An instrument can convey title subject to specifically named right-of-way declarations, while cover and transfer-form descriptions disagree; field-specific source ranking and named-burden assertions cannot depend on the registration label. |
| 2002122700123001 | MORTGAGE | A revolving variable-rate home-equity instrument states a $40,000 maximum/credit limit without stating every rate term and may address subordination, so maximum lien, credit limit, balance, new money, priority, and absent terms must remain separate. |
| 2002122700179002 | ASSIGNMENT MORTGAGE | The operative effect is transfer from assignor to assignee even when a representative signs; the referenced mortgage's $295,000 original principal is not current balance or assignment consideration. |
| 2002123000036001 | AGREEMENT | A subordination agreement can change only the priority between a $50,000 lien and a prospective $108,600 lien, without modifying either debt's principal or creating new capital. |
| 2003010400020001 | UCC3 TERMINATION | A UCC continuation/addendum can name an additional debtor and the filing itself can be the operative termination act; termination of filing effectiveness does not prove payment of the secured obligation. |
| 2003010500014002 | ASSIGNMENT OF LEASE | Assignment of the full unexpired leasehold term, assumed duties, a unit, and a 0.2337% common interest require Title transfer, typed lease terms, party direction, and appurtenant-interest handling separate from the $825,000 transaction value. |
| 2003010500041001 | INITIAL UCC1 | A fixture filing may describe broad collateral and real-estate location while the underlying security agreement's creation date remains unseen; filing date is operative only for the filing event. |
| 2003010600008001 | AGREEMENT | A correction can replace a note/mortgage maturity date from August 1 to July 1, 2032 while leaving the debt and security alive and creating no new indebtedness, requiring old/new correction history and narrowly scoped deltas. |
| 2003010600046001 | DEED OTHER | One transfer can affect two BBLs with a joint $340,000 total and a joint $6,800 assessment that the document does not allocate, so fanning must conserve a total instead of duplicating or dividing it. |
| 2003010600065002 | EASEMENT | A perpetual facade easement over a partial BBL simultaneously burdens land and constrains physical form, with maintenance, access, casualty thresholds, and conditional termination as present terms rather than future events. |
| 2003010600302005 | LEASE | A filing labeled LEASE may actually assign and assume a 99-year ground lease for the remaining term with nominal $10 consideration, proving that operative clauses, not registration type, select mode and effect. |
| 2003010601328004 | DECLARATION | A declaration amendment can assign parking spaces 1–4 as restricted common elements to four named units while leaving spaces 5–8 unrestricted, even though the cover fans across sixteen condo BBLs; operative parcel subsets and common-interest Title effects must control. |
| 2003010801527001 | ZONING LOT DESCRIPTION | A private title-company certification can assert present zoning-lot composition and exact 100-by-25-foot geometry without granting development rights or acting as a governmental permit, sharpening the Identity/Entitlement/Permit boundary. |
