## ⚠ WHAT THIS TABLE DOES NOT PROVE

`span outstanding 0` is measured ABOVE the baseline. The gap is computed as `range(baseline+1, edge+1)`, so **holes BELOW the baseline are never examined** — and `document` stores no CRFN, so the map cannot answer which recording numbers it holds.

Measured 2026-08-20: 2026 issued **235,036** CRFNs; the map holds **234,972** ACRIS documents recorded in 2026 — a difference of **64**, an upper bound (some issued numbers never resolve).

Closing it needs `crfn` on `document`, then `held` computed from the map instead of `_live_delta_queue.jsonl` (which carries one month, not the year).
