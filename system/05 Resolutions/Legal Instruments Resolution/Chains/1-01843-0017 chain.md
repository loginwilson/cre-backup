## GRAMMAR AUDIT

- **Title continuity: 5 of 6 links close.** Each grantor is the prior grantee.
- **NAMED BREAK (1981-05-22 -> 1983-03-13):** grantee of record was FIRST
  FUNDING EQUITY *c/o* CROSSROADS PROPERTIES LTD; the next grantor is
  CROSSROADS PROPERTIES LTD itself. Either the care-of party was the true
  principal or a conveyance is missing. Candidates: (a) c/o party = principal,
  (b) unrecorded/missing deed, (c) name change. **Not silently joined.**

  handwritten and was unreadable in isolation; the 1983 deed's typed grantor
  is BENSON HOLDING CORP, and title continuity forces the match. The chain
  read what the page could not. (New rail: R-002.)
- **KEY DIVERGENCE 1984:** documents 1-5 convey Lots 17 AND 51 together; the
  1984 deed conveys **Lot 17 only** (165 Manhattan Ave). From here the two
  lots' chains separate — the multi-key case, live.
