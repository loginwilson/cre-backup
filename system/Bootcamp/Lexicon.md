# THE LEXICON — class-level definitions earned from documents

THE CONTEXT LINE (Bootcamp.md) permits importing knowledge that lands on
a CLASS and forbids knowledge that lands on the INSTANCE. Class-level
knowledge is therefore a LOOKUP, not a recollection — and this is the
table it looks up. A summary sentence may cite (a) a row, (b) a
computation over rows, or (c) AN ENTRY HERE. Nothing else.

Every entry was earned by reading an instrument of that class in a
bootcamp run — the run is cited so the definition can be re-checked
against the document that taught it. **Never add an entry from memory
alone; add it when a document teaches it.**

| term | class-level meaning | earned |
|---|---|---|
| **CEMA** (Consolidation, Extension and Modification Agreement) | Glues an existing mortgage and new money into a single lien; NY mortgage recording tax is then charged only on the new money, evidenced by a Tax Law §255 affidavit filed with it | R13 |
| **§255 affidavit** | Sworn statement filed with a consolidation reciting tax already paid on the prior mortgage and tax due on new money; it is the exemption's paperwork and the price-witness for the tax | R13 |
| **purchase money mortgage** | A mortgage given to fund the purchase it secures | R20 |
| **balloon** | Payments computed on a longer amortization than the term, leaving a lump sum due at maturity | R20 |
| **rate floor** | A minimum rate below which an adjustable rate cannot fall | R20 |
| **non-recourse (exculpation)** | Lender's remedy limited to the property; no deficiency or personal judgment. Common carve-outs: environmental, fraud | R11, R20 |
| **assignment of leases and rents (AL&R)** | Landlord's rights to tenant rents pledged as collateral; typically absolute with a revocable licence back to the borrower until default | R20, R21 |
| **fixture filing (UCC1 in land records)** | Security interest in equipment/fixtures attached to realty, recorded in the county REAL-ESTATE records with block and lot | R28, R32 |
| **UCC continuation** | Financing statements lapse at five years; a continuation filed in the pre-lapse window extends effectiveness. NO filing = silent death | R32 |
| **UCC termination** | Secured party's statement that it no longer claims the security interest. Proves RELEASE of the claim, NOT that the debt was paid | R30 |
| **co-op share loan** | Loan secured by shares in a cooperative corporation plus the proprietary lease — personal property, so it appears in UCC filings, never in mortgage records | R30 |
| **co-op underlying mortgage** | The cooperative CORPORATION's own building debt — a separate floor from unit-owners' share loans | R32 |
| **satisfaction of mortgage** | Instrument discharging a mortgage as paid; the satisfier warrants it still holds the note ("not further assigned of record"), which is its validity condition | R25 |
| **partial release** | Releases specific parcels from a lien while the rest remains security; may be ceremonial ($1) or priced | R14, R15 |
| **subordination** | Changes the RANK of a lien, not its amount, parties or term. Executed by the yielding party alone; typically capped at the senior lien's amount plus interest and advances | G-037, R26, R36 |
| **memorandum of lease** | Recorded notice of a lease stating parties, premises, term and options but NOT rent, by design | R16 |
| **sublease-back** | The same premises leased in both directions between the same two parties, often same-day; both leases are live simultaneously | R35 |
| **restrictive covenant scheme** | Developer-imposed use/build rules binding successors; may sunset by DATE, by EVENT, or run perpetually | R12, R24, R29, R34 |
| **utility easement (blanket)** | A utility's recorded right to install and maintain service lines across described land; typically runs with the land, may bind lots as later subdivided or consolidated, and may allow the grantee to admit other utilities | R38 |
| **private utility charge covenant** | Where a private plant (sewer, water) serves a parcel, the owner records a covenant obliging an annual charge that becomes a lien on default and binds successors; typically sunsets on an EVENT (municipal service arrives, or the municipality acquires the plant) | R34, R39 |
| **covenant running with the land** | An obligation attaching to the parcel, binding every future owner regardless of who signed | R29, R34 |
| **tax lien certificate (NYC)** | The City sells delinquent property-tax debt; the lien is assigned into a trust and collected by a custodian. Certificate = distress event; discharge = recovery event | R31 |
| **estate tax lien** | Attaches automatically at death by statute — no instrument is recorded creating it; its RELEASE is the record's only sighting | R27 |
| **Letters of Administration** | Surrogate's Court authority for an administrator to act for an estate, including assigning a decedent's mortgage to distributees | R33 |
| **"subject to" (in a deed)** | Title transfers while a recited lien survives intact; the recital also proves the lien was alive on that date | R22 |
| **bargain and sale w/ covenant** | Grantor warrants only against his OWN acts, not prior owners' | R17, R18 |
| **warranty deed** | Carries the five full covenants (seizin, quiet enjoyment, free of encumbrances, further assurance, warranty) — identifiable by the covenant ladder, not the title line | R19 |
| **documentary stamp (federal)** | Pre-1968 conveyance tax evidenced by stamps affixed to the deed; the denomination is the era's only price witness | R19, R22 |
| **filed map** | A subdivision plan recorded with a county registry; its lot numbers are their own namespace and may or may not match tax lots | R12, R19, R23, R24 |
| **computor pump** | (Document's own spelling.) A gasoline pump with a built-in mechanical calculator multiplying gallons by price | R35 |
| **sidewalk waiver / WAIVER OF LEGAL GRADE (NYC)** | Owner's recorded covenant waiving claims against the City and promising future sidewalk conformance, given to obtain the consent of the Commissioner of Transportation (earlier: Highways) to issuance of a Certificate of Occupancy by the Commissioner of Buildings. R51 adds the machinery from City form MP-53A: the trigger is the Commissioner's REFUSAL to certify under Charter §2904 and General City Law §36, whose only alternative is a Board of Standards and Appeals appeal; §27-204 covers the case where a sidewalk is certified NOT REQUIRED; the covenant runs with the land, reaches the intersection quadrant on corner property, is performable whenever the Commissioner directs, and requires security | R29, R51 |
| **securitization trust as holder** | "X Bank as trustee for [Series]" — the beneficial owners are certificate holders; the actionable party is the servicer, who is not of record | R28 |
| **servicer** | The company that collects payments and administers a loan on behalf of whoever owns it; ownership and servicing are separate roles and either can change hands independently | R28, R37 |
| **limited power of attorney (servicing)** | A loan owner appoints its servicer attorney-in-fact to execute enumerated instrument types (satisfactions, releases, modifications, subordinations, foreclosure papers) portfolio-wide; scope is by loan population, not by parcel | R37 |
| **attorney-in-fact** | An entity or person authorized to sign in another's name; the actual human signer is typically a designated employee of the attorney-in-fact, two delegations from the principal | R37 |
| **foreign certified copy (re-recording)** | An instrument recorded in one jurisdiction, certified a true copy by that clerk, then recorded again elsewhere; the local document number is not the original's cite | R37 |
| **agent for co-lenders** | A named lender acting for undisclosed participants; the co-lenders are not in the record at all | R25 |
| **RP-5217 (NYC)** | State real-property transfer report filed WITH a deed and imaged as a supporting document behind the main instrument. Carries the FULL SALE PRICE, contract date, use category, assessed value and both signers — none of which the deed itself states. It is a separate document with its own cover page, so it falls OUTSIDE the main document's page count | R40 |
| **party wall** | A wall standing on the boundary and serving both adjoining buildings; metes descriptions run "part of the distance through a party wall," which locates the line INSIDE the wall rather than at its face | R40 |
| **dominant / servient estate** | An easement's benefited parcel (dominant) and burdened parcel (servient). Each side is recited in its own parcel's description; both recitals must be read to place the burden | R40 |
| **BEING clause** | A deed's closing recital naming the instrument by which the grantor took title ("being the same premises conveyed to the party of the first part by deed dated X recorded Y in Reel R Page P"). It is the chain-back edge, and it cites in the REEL/PAGE namespace, not CRFN or document id | R40 |
| **two-thirds shareholder consent recital** | A corporate grantor's recital that holders of at least two-thirds of outstanding voting shares consented at a duly called meeting; it is the authority element for the conveyance, appearing in the deed rather than as a separate instrument | R40 |
| **condo declaration (RPL Art 9-B)** | The instrument that submits a parcel to condominium ownership: re-cuts one tax lot into unit tax lots plus undivided common-element interests, binds every unit with covenants running with the land (including the common-charge lien machinery), and attaches by-laws. Indexed under the CREATED condominium's name; keyed to the pre-existing lot | R41 |
| **common interest (condominium)** | Each unit's undivided percentage of the common elements — the divisor for charges, votes, and condemnation awards; fixed in the declaration, changeable only with consent of all affected owners; inseparable from the unit | R41 |
| **limited / restricted common element** | A common-element area (yard, balcony, storage) irrevocably restricted in USE to one unit — restricted use, never fee; its SF is not part of the unit's fee SF | R41 |
| **offering plan acceptance (CPS letter)** | NY Attorney General letter accepting a sponsor's offering literature for filing (GBL Art 23-A); states the offering AMOUNT (asking for all units), effective 12 months unless extended; an act about the OFFERING, not the parcel | R41 |
| **heter iska** | (Document usage: "interest at the highest legal rate al pi heter iska," qualifying what a condo board may collect on arrears.) The document uses but does not define the term; imported class gloss, marked as such: a rabbinic-law structure governing interest-bearing obligations. The verbatim phrase is the banked fact; this gloss is context only | R41 |
| **drainage/shared-infrastructure declaration** | A declaration of easement, maintenance and restrictions that DOB/DEP require as a condition precedent to a sewer/water permit where buildings share infrastructure on one lot: grants the utility easement, binds owner and successors to maintain the lines per the filed drainage plan, indemnifies the City; runs with the land, typically sunsetting when an approved HOA/condominium takes the facilities over. May be shelved under unrelated type codes ("DECLARATION OF MERGER") | R42 |
| **single-ownership covenant** | A recorded covenant that the premises may not be subdivided and must be held in single ownership until named condo/HOA papers are approved and recorded — a gate on the lot's own future identity, living in the encumbrance layer | R42 |
| **merger of estates** | When one owner comes to hold both a lease (or other lesser estate) and the fee, the lesser estate is extinguished by operation of law — no instrument records the death, so the lease stays record-alive until a later declaratory filing witnesses it. (Mechanism shown operationally by R43's recitals: tenant interests + fee acquired by one corporation → interests "merged upon acquisition" → leases "terminated") | R43 |
| **declaration of merger (register shelf)** | ⚠ A type code, not a function: measured 0-for-2 as a lot-merger source — held a shared-drainage declaration (R42) and an estates-merger declaration (R43). Read the instrument's own title line | R42, R43 |
| **amended condo declaration (unit re-cut)** | Amendment by the declarant/unit owner under the declaration's own amendment article that subdivides or combines units — minting and retiring unit tax lots while total unit count and 100% common interest are preserved; the revised Exhibit B replaces the old wholesale, and the register keys the full before-and-after lot set. Common-interest percentages conserve exactly (the legal currency); sponsor-asserted SF may not | R44 |
| **building loan mortgage / project loan split** | NY Lien Law two-tier construction financing: the BUILDING LOAN mortgage secures advances for the improvement itself (filed with a building loan contract, §22); the PROJECT LOAN mortgage secures everything else (soft costs, acquisition). Recorded as coordinate series (I, II, III…) with the building-loan tier SENIOR to the project-loan tier by agreement — one lender, one site, a deliberate lien ladder | R45 |
| **consolidation agreement (CEMA)** | Consolidates existing mortgages plus a new gap mortgage into a single lien for the combined principal; NY mortgage recording tax (2.25% commercial in the read era) is paid on the NEW money only — the schedule of existing mortgages (each with its own tax stamp) is the tax-free rollover's proof. The consolidated amount = opening balance + new advance, checkable to the dollar (G-022) | R45 |
| **mortgage spreader (spreader agreement)** | Instrument spreading an existing mortgage's lien onto ADDITIONAL property (or additional estates in the same property) without new money — the lien grows in reach, not in principal; appears inside recited chains when a loan's collateral footprint widened | R45 |
| **deconsolidation** | The inverse of consolidation: one consolidated mortgage split back into separately assignable liens (e.g. $120M → $110M + $10M) so the pieces can be assigned, restructured, or re-consolidated into a new series; no new money, no new tax | R45 |
| **anti-merger covenant** | Mortgage/lease clause providing that the fee and leasehold shall NOT merge notwithstanding union of the estates in one holder — contracting out of merger-of-estates (see that entry); often paired with an automatic lien-spread onto the after-acquired fee. Whether a union of estates actually merges them is governed by such recorded covenants, not by the union alone | R45 |
| **zoning lot development agreement (ZLDA)** | The private contract half of an air-rights deal: transfers the seller's excess floor-area development rights, allocates future FAR changes, caps rebuilds, and binds both sides' construction/DOB conduct; runs with the land. Recorded WITH the §12-10(d) declaration that makes the zoning-lot merger effective — the ZLDA sets the terms, the declaration changes the map | R46 |
| **declaration of zoning lot restrictions (§12-10(d))** | The declaration by all "parties in interest" (fee owners, mortgagees, lessees — those not waiving) that named contiguous tax lots in one block shall be treated as a SINGLE ZONING LOT under ZR §12-10; recorded, runs with the land, survives breach of any side agreement, undone only by ZR-compliant subdivision. The operative instrument of a zoning-lot merger | R46 |
| **single zoning lot vs tax lot** | A declared zoning lot is a second identity layer: one envelope container over several tax lots that remain separate for ownership, conveyance and taxation — the record itself legislates the divergence (R46's Art V prices its tax consequences). FAR compliance is computed on the zoning lot; nothing about the tax-lot map shows it | R46 |
| **floor area development rights / excess development rights** | The buildable floor area a zoning lot's FAR allows minus what its buildings use; the excess is transferable to a neighbor within a merged zoning lot. Quantity claims are typically one party's determination (R46: Developer-determined, Owner no-representation) — a declarant figure, not a certification | R46 |
| **light-and-air easement** | A perpetual negative easement over a described area of the burdened parcel forbidding new structures above carve-outs, appurtenant to the benefited parcel — the physical guarantee that purchased FAR stays usable (windows keep legal light and air). Frequently rides INSIDE air-rights instruments (R46-3: functions-not-type-owned) | R46 |
| **parties in interest / waiver (§12-10(d))** | Every holder of an interest in a would-be zoning lot (fee, mortgage, lease) must join the declaration OR execute a recorded waiver; the declaration's recital of waiving parties implies recorded waiver instruments exist as chain siblings | R46 |
| **directors'/secretary's certificate (corporate authority)** | Officer-sworn certification of board resolutions authorizing a transaction, delivered to induce the lender and title insurer; recorded alongside the instruments it authorizes. Creates no lien and conveys nothing (cover taxes $0) — its cargo is authority: who may bind the entity, certified by whom | R47 |
| **incumbency certificate / specimen signatures** | The certified officer roster: each office, each holder, each genuine signature — recorded proof of who the entity's humans are and what their signatures look like; the cheap officer-roster source and a cross-source human join key | R47 |
| **authorized vs issued shares** | A charter grants AUTHORITY TO ISSUE N shares (a ceiling); how many are actually issued lives in stock records/offering plan. Per-share arithmetic (votes, maintenance, value) needs the ISSUED count — the charter number alone licenses nothing | R47 |
| **co-op purpose clause (BCL charter)** | A housing co-op's certificate of incorporation states the machinery in its purpose clause: shareholders, solely by reason of stock ownership, hold proprietary leases for apartment occupancy — the co-op structure READ from a founding document rather than inferred from a corporate name | R47 |
| **good-standing certificate (DOS)** | NYS Department of State certification that a corporation subsists: incorporation date, no dissolution on file, optionally the biennial-statement trail — a state-certified entity timeline that can arrive inside an ACRIS recording | R47 |
| **revolving line of credit mortgage** | A mortgage securing a revolving credit note up to a stated maximum — the lien caps at the authorized sum while the drawn balance moves; paired here with a CMEA consolidating existing debt (see consolidation agreement) | R47 |
| **waiver and consent agreement** | An instrument in which parties holding rights that could block a transaction consent to it and release those rights — creating and conveying nothing itself, but recorded because the released rights ran with the land. Its cargo is permission; the conveyances live in companion deeds | R48 |
| **right of first offer (statutory)** | A preemptive right to purchase before the owner may sell elsewhere, here created by federal statute rather than contract (Balanced Budget Act of 1997 §9101(b)) and held by governments. Invisible in any deed chain until an instrument records its exercise or waiver. ⚠ Model question queued: public-law origin (ENTITLEMENT) vs private-law effect (ENCUMBRANCE on transferability) | R48 |
| **national monument / Antiquities Act designation** | A presidential proclamation under the Antiquities Act of 1906 (16 U.S.C. 431) establishing a national monument on federal land; a public-law layer attaching to the parcel, with boundaries fixed by a further proclamation and a numbered boundary map | R48 |
| **counterpart execution** | Each party signs its own copy; all copies together are one instrument. In the record this appears as several signature pages carrying the SAME page number, each bearing exactly one signature — read them all: the party list is the signature pages, not the index | R48 |
| **perpetuities savings clause (measuring lives)** | A clause capping any provision that would otherwise violate the rule against perpetuities at 21 years after the death of the last survivor of a named group living at execution. The named people are a MEASURING DEVICE — never parties, never related to the parties | R48 |
| **letters patent** | The instrument by which a sovereign conveys land it holds — in New York, the People of the State granting through a named commissioner under a statute that authorises the disposal. A root of a chain rather than a link in one; it recites its own statutory warrant, is approved by the Attorney General and Comptroller before it issues, and carries the Great Seal | R49 |
| **Book of Patents (state custodian)** | The New York Department of State's own recording series for letters patent, numbered by book and page and stamped by the Secretary of State. A SECOND CUSTODIAN alongside the county register: an instrument may be recorded there months before (or instead of) any local recording, so a corpus built only from city records can carry the wrong date or miss the document entirely | R49 |
| **appropriation map** | The instrument by which the State takes land for a public purpose: a titled map and numbered parcel filed with the county recording officer, naming the acquiring department and program. Vests title without a deed — a taking is findable only by its map citation | R49 |
| **party wall as boundary monument** | A property description may run its course *through* a shared wall, using the wall to locate the line rather than to grant rights in it. ⚠ Read as a monument: describing a party wall creates no party-wall easement — the mutual rights, if any, come from a separate instrument or from law | R49 |
| **public benefit corporation** | A corporate governmental agency created by state statute to carry out a public purpose, holding property in its own name and able to take a conveyance directly from the State; a distinct grantee from the State itself even when the State is its sole principal | R49 |
| **legally established lines and grades / City Map** | The City Map fixes the official horizontal line and vertical grade of every mapped street; building a sidewalk, curb or roadway to anything else requires either a certification that conforms, a waiver, or a BSA appeal. The obligation to conform later can outlive the construction and bind successors | R51 |
| **certification refusal (Charter §2904 / General City Law §36)** | Before a Certificate of Occupancy issues, the Transportation Commissioner certifies to the Buildings Commissioner that sidewalk, curbs and roadway meet specification. A REFUSAL to certify is the trigger event: it forces either a BSA appeal or a recorded waiver, and only the waiver leaves a land record | R51 |
| **type-shelf mismatch (defined)** | ⚠ The register's document type MISDESCRIBING the instrument (r42, r48, r50, r51). NOT the same as a type being accurate while the reader's expected cell failed to materialise (r43, r46) — that is the reader's expectation missing, not the register's label | R42, R48, R50, R51 |
| **quitclaim deed / donation deed pair** | A conveyance passing whatever interest the grantor has, without warranty, often for nominal consideration; paired here with a donation deed passing the same property onward for no consideration — a two-step routing of title through an intermediary holder | R48 |

## WHAT THIS TABLE IS NOT

It holds no fact about any particular parcel, person, deal, or purpose.
The moment an entry would need a name or a date to be true, it belongs
in a row or a claim, not here.
