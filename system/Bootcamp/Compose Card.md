# THE COMPOSE CARD — read this BEFORE writing any verdict sentence

Short by design. Bootcamp.md is the authority; this is the card that
fires at the moment of composing. A rule that is not loaded at the right
moment does not exist.

## 1 · EVERY SENTENCE HAS A TIER, AND YOU MUST KNOW WHICH

    READ      the document says it (anchor: page + line)
    DERIVED   follows necessarily — arithmetic · calendar · legal
              consequence of a visible fact · definitional decode of an
              instrument class or term of art
    INFERRED  purpose · relationship · characterization · industry or
              era practice → MARK IT IN THE SENTENCE or DELETE IT

Tier 2 carries essentially all narrative value and is fully safe.
**If a story only lands when you supply motive, the story is wrong or
the facts are not finished.**

## 2 · THE CLASS/INSTANCE TEST (apply to any imported knowledge)

Does this land on a CLASS or on THIS INSTANCE?

    CLASS  → safe. "A CEMA glues an old mortgage and new money into one
             lien." "A co-op owner holds shares + a proprietary lease."
    INSTANCE → stop. "She couldn't write." "Her three heirs." "How the
             oil majors did business." All three were failures.

## 3 · EFFECT, NEVER INTENT

    YES  "mortgage tax was $331.54 instead of ~$11,000"
    YES  "the escape clause is struck, so the fifteen years is firm"
    NO   "they did it to avoid the tax" · "Texaco wanted control"

The product wants the number, not the intention.

## 4 · TRIGGER WORDS — stop and anchor or mark

    "was how" · "because" · "in order to" · "so that" · "which is why"
    "typically" · "at the time" · "in those days" · "the practice was"
    any relationship word (wife, son, family, partner, related)
    any role word not printed on the page (manager, operator, developer)
    any motive ("wanted to", "needed to", "so they could")

## 5 · PEOPLE vs ENTITIES

An officer is never shorthand for his corporation. The lessor is the
company; the president is a person who signed for it.

## 6 · KEEP THE INSTRUMENT'S OWN NOUN

"residential units" ≠ "houses". "computor pumps" ≠ "pumps". If a word
carries arithmetic or is a term of art, quote it and teach it in place.

## 7 · RECONCILIATION MARKS ARE EARNED

✓ only when TWO independent readings agree. When the document-side
reading is too weak to confirm, write "accepted — rd sole witness".
rd never upgrades unread → read.

## 8 · LENGTH

Summary 3-6 sentences. Table is the table. Claims are a GATED list
(fills a slot / chain edge / changes a decision). A verdict longer than
its predecessor on a similar document is DRIFT — scaffolding is leaking
into product. Corrections SHORTEN; the diff goes to the log, never the
deliverable.

## 9 · IF IT CANNOT BE DONE, SAY SO

"Too faint to read" is a deliverable. Unknown beats wrong, always.
On degraded artifacts, the high-dpi second look is MANDATORY for:
stamps · identity strings (address, block/lot) · **term lines** ·
signature and date lines. A deletion on a term line changes the ROW.
