## 3 · RESOLUTION

**1 · ⚠ THE INDEX TYPE IS THE INVERSE OF THE EFFECT.** `doc_type = LEAS` on a document
whose entire purpose is to **terminate** a lease. Not a wrong label — a **sign error**.

> A pipeline keyed on `LEAS` **opens** an occupancy interest where this document **closes**
> one. The parcel would carry a phantom IDA leasehold from 1986 to today.

This is the doc-type rule at its sharpest: the type carries **no sign**. `LEAS` covers
create, amend and terminate alike. **Only the operative verb carries direction**, and
`terminates` is a mode the register must hold as a first-class value, not a flavour of AGMT.

**2 · One document, three closures and one opening.** A sale-leaseback unwind is braided:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| TITLE | Agency holds fee | **returns to the Company** |
| OCCUPANCY | Company leases from Agency | **lease terminated** |
| CAPITAL | $7.5M IDA bonds outstanding | **redeemed and retired** |

All three resolve from **one** operative clause plus its incorporated purchase-price
mechanism. They are one event with three effects, not three events.

**3 · ⚠ SURVIVAL IS NOT ENCUMBRANCE.** Six Lease sections survive termination — and §1
states they are *"personal to the Company and do not run with the land."* **A surviving
obligation that does not run with the land must never post as a parcel encumbrance.** The
document supplies the test itself; nothing has to be inferred.

**4 · A term can EXPIRE.** §4's notice covenant runs *"from the date hereof to May 31,
1990."* First dated-expiry term in the library. **A term with no end date field encumbers
forever** — this one was dead 36 years ago.

**5 · The reel stamp is unreadable and does not need to be read.** The degraded stamp reads
`2160` on some pages and `2100` on others — OCR split across the 11 pages and my own visual
read of p001 said `2100`. **`master.reel_nbr` = 2160.** Settled from the index, not the
image.

**6 · The recording date: OCR said 1996, the truth is 1986.** OCR produced `AUG211996` from
the register stamp. Three separate `08-21-86` stamps on the same page, and
`master.recorded_datetime = 1986-08-21`, agree. **A ten-year error, caught by repetition and
by the index.**

## 4 · DERIVATION

**Queens Block 11, Lot 1 — waterfront, Long Island City**

In **1983** the New York City Industrial Development Agency issued **$7.5 million** of
tax-exempt industrial development bonds in two series to finance a beer-and-beverage
warehouse for **Port Distributing Corp.** — buying three existing buildings, renovating
them, adding an extension and office space, and installing the equipment. Under the standard
structure the Agency **took title** to the property and **leased it back** to the company,
which is how the tax exemption works.

On **22 July 1986** the company paid off the bonds early and unwound the deal. It paid the
trustee enough to redeem every outstanding bond plus interest, covered the agency's fees,
and paid **one dollar**. The lease was terminated, and the property came back to the company
free of the agency's interest. Recorded **21 August 1986**.

The agreement also cleared the way for a transfer to **H. Dieter Holterbosch** personally,
waiving the agency's recapture rights so long as he or the company owned the whole facility.

**What it means**

- **The IDA's interest in this parcel ended in 1986.** Anything showing an agency leasehold
  after that date is stale.
- **The $7.5M of public financing is retired**, not outstanding.
- **The six surviving obligations do not attach to the land** — the document says so
  explicitly. A buyer takes clean of them.
- **The notice covenant expired 31 May 1990.** Nothing from this agreement binds the parcel
  today.
- **Ownership consolidated toward Holterbosch personally** — the Beck's beer importing
  family. An owner-user unwinding public financing and taking direct title is a *long-hold*
  signal, not a sale signal.

⚠ **The recorded price on this document is effectively $1, and the deal was $7.5 million.**
Any comparable analysis reading consideration off the index gets this catastrophically
wrong.

### CODE

| rule | check | measured |
|---|---|---|
| **⚠ `doc_type` carries no SIGN** | never derive open/close from the type. `LEAS` covers create, amend **and terminate** | this document |
| **Reel/page from the index, never the image** | `master.reel_nbr` / `reel_pg` | stamp read `2160`/`2100` split across 11 pages; index says 2160 |
| **Recording date from the index, never the stamp** | `master.recorded_datetime` | OCR said `1996`; truth `1986-08-21` — a **10-year** error |

| **`document_amt` is not the quantity** | index amount `0`; instrument magnitude `$7,500,000` | this document |
| **A term needs an expiry field** | §4 runs to a stated date | expired 1990-05-31, still readable as live without the field |
| **FT_ microfilm is not slower** | 3.9 s/page vs 4.9–5.3 modern | 11 pages, 1,205 lines, 42.8 s |

### MODEL

- **`terminates` is a first-class mode.** Without it a ledger only ever grows. The operative
  verb — not the doc type — carries direction.
- **⚠ The `$1` is a COMPONENT, not the price.** *"(c) one dollar"* is the third term of a
  formula whose magnitude is a bond redemption. Same family as the $10 recital trap, new
  costume: here the nominal figure is **structurally correct and still meaningless alone.**
- **Survival ≠ encumbrance.** *"personal to the Company and do not run with the land"* is the
  document telling you not to post it against the parcel.
- **One operative clause can close three functions.** A sale-leaseback unwind closes
  OCCUPANCY and CAPITAL and moves TITLE. Do not split into three events; do not collapse to one function.

### HUMAN

- **⚠ Metes with interior angles and a 1916 Pierhead & Bulkhead line.** *"an interior angle
  of 127 degrees, 51 minutes, 17.2 seconds with the former center line of 5th Street"* —
  exactly the class `metes.py` refuses (23 of 31 Greenpoint descriptions). Waterfront parcels
  bounded by a **federal bulkhead line** cannot be walked from courses alone; the boundary is
  an external authority, not a bearing.
- Two exhibit descriptions (54th Avenue and 55th Avenue frontages) against one asserted lot.
