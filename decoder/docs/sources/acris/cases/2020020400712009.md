## 3 · RESOLUTION

**1 · Two documents, one event.** The affidavit creates no obligation and moves nothing. It
is the **warrant for a quantity**, not a sibling event. Folds into event 1; its `attests`
mode survives as provenance for the exemption.

**2 · The "$80,000,000 vs $79,586,625.11 conflict" is not a conflict — it is a field error.**

| slot | value | said by |
|---|---|---|
| prior **face** | 80,000,000.00 | Recital B *"original principal aggregate amount"*; both schedules |
| prior **balance** | **79,586,625.11** | Recital B *"on which there is now owing"* |

The affidavit ¶1 wrote the face into the balance sentence. **Separate the slots and the
conflict evaporates.** Both claims are then true.

**3 · A consolidation is a REPLACEMENT, not an addition.**

```
post 85,500,000 as new debt     -> inflates the parcels by 79.6M
post  5,913,374.89 as new debt  -> loses the 79.6M still owed
correct: CLOSE 79,586,625.11 · OPEN 85,500,000.00 · net +5,913,374.89
```

⚠ The City is an **external witness to the accounting**: it taxed only the new money.

**4 · The creditor change is asserted here, effected elsewhere.** Recital C: Capital One
assigned to Helaba *"by assignment dated as of the date hereof… intended to be recorded
simultaneously herewith."* A different instrument. Recorded as **asserted, pending its own
filing** — never as effected.

**5 · Partial Lot — resolved FALSE, and not by an ACRIS document.**

| | deed's own courses | DOF recorded lot area | apart |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lot 1 | 125'0" × 201'5" = **25,177.08 sf** | **25,177** | 0.0003% |
| Lot 45 | 380'0" × 100'8½" = **38,269.17 sf** | **38,270** | 0.0022% |

Two independent exact matches: **the mortgage covers the entire tax lot in both cases.**
The flag is a preparer's checkbox on a page whose own header limits it to *"indexing
purposes"*; it is almost certainly inherited from the 1977–2008 chain, when this debt did
span six lots across Manhattan and the Bronx and was carved by severance and splitter
agreements.

⚠ **THE DTM POLYGON DID NOT ANSWER THIS AND WOULD HAVE MISLED.** Raw polygon area read
4.4% / 3.6% above the deed. Calibrated on **2,057 UWS lots**, DTM-polygon-area ÷
DOF-lot-area has **median 1.0349**, p25 1.0070, p75 1.0708, and only **38% land within
±3%**. Our two lots sit mid-distribution — ordinary. **The geometry store is not usable for
absolute area without calibration**, and anything computing buildable SF off it runs ~3.5%
high by default.

## 4 · DERIVATION

**70 & 50 West 93rd Street · Manhattan Block 1206, Lots 1 and 45 · apartment buildings**

On **23 December 2019** the owners refinanced. They already owed **$79,586,625.11** to
Capital One across five mortgages going back to 1977. Capital One sold that debt to
**Landesbank Hessen-Thüringen (Helaba)**, a German state bank. Helaba lent **$5,913,374.89
of new money** and rolled everything into **one $85,500,000 mortgage** covering both
buildings together. Recorded **5 February 2020**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Owed before | $79,586,625.11 |
| New cash | $5,913,374.89 |
| **Owed after** | **$85,500,000.00** |
| Mortgage tax paid | $165,575.20 |
| **Tax avoided by structuring it this way** | **$2,228,424.80** |

Tax was paid on the new $5.9M instead of the full $85.5M — **a $2.23M saving**, which is why
it was done as a consolidation rather than a fresh mortgage.

**What it means**

- **One owner, not two.** Two LLCs, one signatory, joint and several. Treat both lots as a
  single holding.
- **The buildings cannot be sold separately** without a lender release. Both stand behind
  the whole $85.5M.
- **Variable rate tied to LIBOR, with a hedge** — a December 2019 LIBOR loan on a 43-year
  debt chain.
- **A German Landesbank replacing a US commercial bank, plus $5.9M of proceeds, is a
  refinance-and-hold posture** — not a prelude to a sale.
- **Balance known, not estimated:** $85,500,000 as of 2020-02-05.

**Not knowable:** rate, maturity, payment schedule. All three live in a Term Loan Agreement
that was never recorded and never will be.

## 5 · RULES LEARNED

### CODE — deterministic, free at corpus scale

| rule | check | measured here |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame count vs cover claim** | `len(frames) == cover.page_count` → if greater, **scan for a second cover page** | 42 vs 29/31; found a supporting-document cover at p032 |
| **Consolidation closure** | `prior_balance + new_advance == principal` | 79,586,625.11 + 5,913,374.89 = 85,500,000.00 **exact** |
| **Tax closure** | components vs `ceil(taxable/100)*100 × statutory rate` | all 5 components and the total **exact to the cent** at 2.800% |
| **⚠ Taxable rounds UP to the next $100** | `ceil(taxable/100)*100` | 5,913,374.89 → 5,913,400. Without this every component is cents-off and looks like OCR damage |
| **Restatement authority ranking** | source instrument > cover/index > restatement in a later schedule | Schedule 1 and Exhibit B **both** say `16,538,384.48`; the source says `.38` |
| **Geometry needs calibration** | never report DTM polygon area absolutely | median bias **+3.5%**, only 38% of 2,057 lots within ±3% |

### MODEL — needs a reader

- **Face vs balance is a QUALIFIER, not a number.** *"original principal aggregate amount"*
  = face; *"on which there is now owing"* = balance. Same document, same paragraph, two
  slots. Getting this wrong is the single highest-consequence error in the type.
- **A consolidation replaces; a mortgage adds.** Read which one the instrument is doing
  before posting anything.
- **An omnibus granting clause is not a title transfer.** *"sold, aliened, enfeoffed,
  conveyed"* appears in a NY mortgage because it is drafted in fee-conveyance language.
  ⚠ Even richer TITLE vocabulary than the 2016 sibling. The **quantity decides, not the noun.**
- **A struck-and-rewritten figure may swap FIELDS, not values.** ~~5,500,000~~ → 5,913,374.89
  replaced *New Indebtedness* with *taxable amount*. A single `amount` column silently
  keeps one and loses the other.

### HUMAN — exception queue

- A closure test fails and the document is not obviously wrong. Here: the affidavit as
  recorded reads `80,000,000 + 5,913,374.89 = 85,913,374.89`, contradicting its own ¶2, the
  instrument, the cover, and the tax. **One root error — the drafter used the face where the
  balance belonged — corrected by hand in ¶3 and never corrected in ¶1.**

  ```
  80,000,000.00 − 79,586,625.11 = 413,374.89
   5,913,374.89 −  5,500,000.00 = 413,374.89     ← identical. One mistake, two symptoms.
  ```

  ⚠ **The arbiter is the tax stamp.** At $5,500,000 the tax would have been $154,000;
  $165,575.20 was collected. **The stamp is the only number in the file a third party
  verified and banked.**
