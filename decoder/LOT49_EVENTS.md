## WHAT THIS PARCEL TAUGHT THAT GENERALISES

1. **Date events by the instrument date.** Lag here ranged 3 days to **7 months**.
2. **A batch is one event** — or two, or three. Summing face across it multiplies
   the truth by up to 18×.
3. **The document type lies routinely** — EASE was twice a ZLDA, SMIS was three
   times a DECL, DEVR was once a confirmation, AGMT is how a CEMA files.
4. **Quantities live in exhibits; prices live on the cover page.** Neither is in
   the index.
5. **The most consequential facts are in recitals** — the rights split, the 1816
   boundary, the 1990 root.
6. **The parcel is not the story's boundary.** The 2008 lot 20 ZLDA and the
   lot 20 airspace price are indexed elsewhere and will never appear here.
7. **A gap in the chain of title is invisible until you list the record by date.**
   The 1998 deed was missing from two drafts of this ledger and nothing flagged
   it — the narrative read perfectly well without it.
