2026-08-22: "we want to use fact to inform narrative. However it's a fine
line between an unbacked assumption and a factual context... it requires
packaging via never passing into an unproven assumption, yet provided
factual context." Then: "you need to assure this is recorded and
understood as you bootcamp."

**Why:** imported knowledge is SAFE on a CLASS ("a CEMA glues an old
mortgage and new money into one lien") and FATAL on the INSTANCE ("her
three heirs", "how the oil majors did business"). Sharper cut: EFFECT is
derivable, INTENT never is — and the product wants the number, not the
intention. Three tiers: READ (page says it) · DERIVED (arithmetic,
calendar, legal consequence, class definition) · INFERRED (mark or
delete). Tier 2 carries essentially all narrative value and is fully
safe; reaching for tier 3 was the cause of every narrative failure.

**How to apply:** `D:\CRE Decoding System\Bootcamp\Compose Card.md` —
nine short checks — is re-read at EVERY compose step, every run, because
the 5,400-line Bootcamp.md cannot be consulted per sentence. This is the
system's own measured failure mode (rules that exist but are
unaddressable when they'd fire: the length law was broken by its author
within the hour). The bootcamp SKILL.md loads the card at step 4.
For the VLM: every generated sentence must cite a row, a computation, or
a lexicon entry — or it does not emit. See [[assumption-law-grading]],
[[decoder-length-law]].
