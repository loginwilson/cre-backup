**OWNER + CONTACT RESOLUTION — the same walk, one link further (operator 2026-07-31): `deed → mortgage → PW1`.** Deed gives the owning ENTITY (grantee); mortgage gives the SPE and the money (borrower = party_type 1, the join); **the PW1 gives the HUMAN — name, company, phone, email, mailing — and is the ONLY one of the three that yields a phone number**, because a natural person must sign it. An LLC can hold title anonymously forever but cannot pull a permit anonymously. ⚠ **The asymmetry that matters: the parcels most interesting as opportunities are exactly the ones with no PW1** — quiet, underbuilt, never filed. Partial fallback for RESIDENTIAL 3+ units is HPD **`feu5-w2e2` Registration Contacts** (`HeadOfficer` / `CorporateOwner` types pierce the LLC; firstname/lastname 82%, business mailing 80%, corporationname 31%) — but it carries **NO phone and NO email**. A quiet NON-residential parcel (vacant lot, warehouse, church) tops out at an entity name + mailing address from the deed.
