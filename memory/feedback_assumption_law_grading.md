2026-08-22 evening, after run 33's "her three heirs" over-claim (the deed
never says heirs): "You really need to make sure not to assume. And if you
do it needs to be very clear... It is worse to overstate a false claim
than say you don't know." And on grading: single runs while monitoring →
deliver report + why-pass, NO self-grade; the user prompts the grade after
(outside-the-momentum grading caught what the self-grade missed).
Overnight/batch → self-grade in full.

**Why:** unmarked inferences are the hallucination risk in person; the
audit over 22 runs found every defect was a RELATIONSHIP or MOTIVE claim
(family, heirship, business role, cause of delay) — the narrative layer's
temptation; quantities/dates/cites survived clean.

**How to apply:** every delivered statement is READ (anchored), VERIFIED
(two witnesses), or INFERRED (marked in the sentence). rd settles faint
readings but never upgrades unread → "✓" — mark "accepted, rd sole
witness." "Too faint to read" is a deliverable. Recorded in Bootcamp.md
(THE ASSUMPTION LAW + GRADING PROTOCOL) and the bootcamp SKILL.md; see
[[bootcamp-batch-format]].

**Overnight rotation:** auto mode never self-grades in the same breath
either — each iteration OPENS by grading the previous run cold (a full
document has displaced its momentum), then draws the next; the night's
last run is left ungraded for the user's morning grade, keeping a human
in the calibration loop.
